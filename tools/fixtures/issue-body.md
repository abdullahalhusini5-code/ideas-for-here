### Idea title

A shared tool shed for the street

### One-line summary

Borrow the drill, not buy it.

### What it is

Most homes on a street own the same drill, ladder and wallpaper steamer, and use each of them twice a year. A shared list of what is on the street, and who to ask, turns that pile of duplicates into one shed.

### Who it helps

Neighbours in flats with nowhere to keep a ladder.

### The honest catch

Someone has to hold the keys, and one broken drill can end the whole thing.

### Area

Community

### How much time

One weekend

### How it is built

Ready-made tools, no code

### What you want out of it

Being useful to people nearby

### First moves

- Ask five neighbours what they would lend.
- Start a shared note with what is on the street.
- Pick one street and one Saturday to test it.

### What you already need

A phone, a spare shelf, one neighbour who will hold a key
