About this page

Small print, in plain English.

A personal, non-commercial page. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to buy.

Privacy

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party scripts. The page stores nothing, not even in your browser: your answers live in memory and vanish when you close the tab. “Copy this brief” writes to your clipboard and nowhere else.

The host, GitHub Pages, keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser, time, page requested — for security and abuse. GitHub's privacy statement covers that. Links to other sites open in a new tab; once you follow one, that site's policy applies.

Licence

The written ideas and the catalog are CC BY 4.0: reuse, adapt, translate, print or sell your version, if you credit “Ideas for Here” and link back.

The code is MIT. Third-party resources linked from the cards stay under their own licences; nothing is copied into this page.

Accessibility

Aiming at WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, 200% zoom, down to 320 px, reduced motion respected, colour never the only signal, and complete kits when printed.

Known limits: cards are rendered by JavaScript, so with scripting off you get the raw data in ideas.js; and the print layout is practical rather than beautiful. If something blocks you, open an issue.

How it is made

One HTML file, one stylesheet, two scripts. No framework, no build step, no webfonts, no photographs, no dependencies — under 100 KB before compression. The matcher is a fixed arithmetic score (area 5, goal 3, time 2, build 2), with its reasons printed on every result. No model, no API, no key.

Ideas come from ordinary local problems; the free pieces come from public catalogs and are linked on each card. Links re-checked by hand and with tools/check-links.js.
